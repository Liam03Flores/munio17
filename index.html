Mission

MUNIO seeks to transform the world through youth by fostering debate, discipline, empathy and teamwork. Through simulated United Nations debates, delegates raise awareness about global challenges and propose solutions. In this way, MUNIO goes beyond academics and inspires future generations.

Vision

MUNIO aims to be recognized in Puebla and nationally, strengthening its prestige after 16 years of continuous improvement. In the short term, it seeks to increase student participation and build academic and professional skills. In the long term, it aspires to a just society where, guided by Jesuit values, life is oriented toward service and responsibility to others.

FAQs

Here are some frequently asked questions about the model. If you have a specific question, you can always contact us by email at mun@example.com.

Can I register if I attend a different high school?

Yes. In fact, most of our delegates come from different high schools across Puebla.

Where can I find the dress code and procedure documents?

In the How to MUN section you will find all the official documents of the model.

How much does it cost and what does it include?

The registration fee is 500 MXN. It includes your delegate kit and full access to the model, including the coffee breaks and the Public Session.

More questions
Do I need previous MUN experience?

No. Many delegates attend their first model with us. The How to MUN section explains the basics so you can arrive prepared.

How do I prepare for my committee?

Research the country or role you will represent, learn about your committee’s topics and review the rules of procedure before the event.

How are committees assigned?

When you register, you choose three committee options. The organizing team will assign your placement and confirm it by email.
