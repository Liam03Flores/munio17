What is MUNIO?

MUNIO (Model United Nations at Instituto Oriente) is an academic experience that brings together students from different schools to debate, negotiate and build solutions to the world's most important challenges. Through dialogue, research and teamwork, we learn to think globally and act locally, just like real diplomats.

Our Mission

MUNIO seeks to transform the world through youth by fostering debate, discipline, empathy and teamwork. Through simulated United Nations debates, delegates raise awareness about global challenges and propose solutions. In this way, MUNIO goes beyond academics and inspires future generations.
